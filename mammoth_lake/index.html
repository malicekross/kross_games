Deep Hole Protocol (65–70 FT)

• Cold Spring Outflow: Thermocline drops sharply to ~68°F.
• Zero Natural Light: Pitch black below 50 FT. High-intensity dive lights mandatory.
• Strict NDL: PADI limit is 40 min at 70 FT. Strictly monitor bottom time.
• Ascent: Staging stop at Platform 6 (22 FT), followed by 3-minute safety stop at 15 FT.

Quarry Diving Rules

• Anti-Silt Kicking: Use frog kicks or modified flutter kicks exclusively. Silt bottom reduces visibility to zero instantly.
• Under-Water Guidelines: Hold guidelines lightly between thumb and forefinger; do not pull.
• Thermocline Notice: Primary temperature drop at 25–30 FT from ~84°F surface to 68°F.
